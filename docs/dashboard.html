Dashboard

A complete dashboard is generated from your configuration. You do not have to use it — every value is a normal entity — but it saves an evening of card wrangling.

Getting it

action: energy_balancer.generate_dashboard
data:
  dashboard_title: Energy
  dashboard_path: energy-balancer

Three things happen:

  1. The two card bundles that ship with the integration are copied into www/energy_balancer/ — eb-cards.js and eb-chain-card-r2.js. They are only rewritten when their content has actually changed.
  2. Both are registered as Lovelace resources, with a content hash appended to the URL so a browser cannot serve a stale copy after an upgrade.
  3. A dashboard is built from your actual cells and registered at the path you gave.

The dashboard reflects your setup: one battery or three, a charger or none. Re-run the service after adding or removing a cell.

What is on it

Flow and status

eb-flow-cardLive energy flow between solar, house, storage, charger and grid.
eb-system-diagram-cardThe chain itself, drawn as cells in order, with each cell's decision.
eb-home-status-cardHouse consumption at a glance.
eb-grid-cardImport, export, and how close you are to the peak ceiling.
eb-solar-card / eb-solar-summary-cardProduction now and today.

Storage

eb-battery-cardState of charge, power and target per battery or pool.
eb-battery-zones-cardThe state-of-charge band — floor, target, ceiling — drawn as zones, which makes it obvious when the target sits below where you are.

Car charging

eb-ev-status-cardConnected, charging, delivering.
eb-ev-progress-cardSession progress.
eb-charger-status-cardThe charger's own reported state.

Money

eb-costs-card / eb-costs-detail-cardCosts, revenue and savings per period.
eb-price-cardThe tariff rates currently in force.
eb-energy-impact-cardWhat the balancing actually saved.
eb-period-selector-cardSwitches the money cards between day, month and year.

Control and planning

eb-control-cardObserver mode, take percentages, floors.
eb-config-cardCurrent configuration, read-only.
eb-load-priority-cardThe chain order — who gets surplus first.
eb-today-plan-cardWhat the forecast implies for the rest of today.
eb-weather-cardConditions, as context for the forecast.
eb-schedule-cardTime-based settings, where you use them.
eb-chart-card / eb-gauge-cardGeneric history and gauge cards in the same visual language.
eb-diagnose-buttonDumps the current chain state for a bug report.

Using the cards yourself

Once the resource is registered, the cards are available anywhere in Home Assistant. Add them by hand in YAML mode: 

type: custom:eb-battery-card
entity: sensor.eb_layer_home_battery

Re-running generate_dashboard overwrites the dashboard at that path. If you have customised it, generate to a different path, or copy your version somewhere safe first.

If the cards do not render

  1. Check that www/energy_balancer/eb-cards.js exists.
  2. Check Settings → Dashboards → Resources for the entry.
  3. Hard-refresh the browser. On the companion app, clear the frontend cache.
  4. On a fresh install, restart Home Assistant once — the registration needs it.
