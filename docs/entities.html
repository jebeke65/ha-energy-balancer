Devices & entities

Everything the integration creates, grouped the way it appears on the integration page.

Devices

Entities are grouped into devices purely to keep the integration page navigable. No entity is renamed by the grouping, and unique ids are unaffected.

DeviceModelHolds
EB SystemChain outputs The umbrella. Chain status, forecast figures, diagnostics, the observer switch, the peak-limit slider. Every other device hangs off this one.
EB <cell>Cell One per cell — including the virtual ones, so EB solar, EB house and EB net exist alongside your batteries and charger. Holds the cell sensor and its take-% slider.
EB Home BatteryBattery pool One per storage pool. Pool power, state of charge, target, charged and discharged energy, and the pool-level sliders and mode select.
EB CostsCost accounting Tariff rates and every cost, saving and revenue figure.

Per-cell sensor

sensor.eb_<cell> — for example sensor.eb_goodwe, sensor.eb_car_charger, sensor.eb_house, sensor.eb_net.

The state is the cell's power in watts, signed: positive means it is taking from the chain, negative means it is giving back. The interesting part is in the attributes — the decision, the reason for it, and what the chain handed this cell to work with.

The actuator reads this sensor, not a service call. Your script is handed the cell sensor and reads the decision from its attributes. That is why adding a device needs no change anywhere in the integration: the contract is a sensor, and every cell publishes the same one.

Pool sensors

Created per storage pool. With a pool called home_battery:

EntityMeaning
sensor.eb_layer_home_batteryCombined power of the pool, in watts.
sensor.eb_layer_home_battery_socCapacity-weighted state of charge across all members.
sensor.eb_layer_home_battery_targetThe target the pool is steering towards right now.
sensor.eb_layer_home_battery_energy_chargedSummed charged energy, kWh.
sensor.eb_layer_home_battery_energy_dischargedSummed discharged energy, kWh.

A pool state of charge can hide an empty member. Two batteries at 25% and 5% average out to something comfortable while one of them has nothing left to give. When a pool looks healthy but the house is still importing, check the members individually.

Costs and accounting

Every figure exists for three periods — daily_, monthly_ and yearly_ — and the counters survive a restart.

SuffixMeaning
_grid_import_energy / _grid_export_energyImported and exported energy, kWh.
_costsCost of imported energy.
_export_revenueRevenue from exported energy.
_net_costCosts minus revenue.
_battery_savingsAvoided import, valued at the rate that applied at the time.
_savingsTotal saving for the period.

Plus sensor.eb_lifetime_total_savings, sensor.eb_tariff_current_import_rate and sensor.eb_tariff_current_export_rate. Rates are applied incrementally, so a tariff that changes during the day is priced correctly rather than retroactively.

Controls

Switch

switch.eb_observer_mode On = compute and publish, never act. Off = live. Writes through to the config entry, so the switch and the options flow always agree.

Numbers

EntityMeaning
number.eb_<cell>_take The take-% slider for one cell. It is a view on the real knob: if take_pct points at an entity, dragging this writes to that entity.
number.eb_<cell>_hysteresisDead band, %.
number.eb_<cell>_charge_floor_wMinimum worthwhile charge power, W.
number.eb_<pool>_sunny_min_socFloor for a sunny forecast. Pool-level.
number.eb_<pool>_no_sun_min_socFloor for a dull forecast. Pool-level.
number.eb_<pool>_manual_min_socThe verbatim floor. Pool-level.
number.eb_peak_limit_wThe fallback import ceiling.

A slider only appears when the value is a number. Configure a parameter as an entity id and no slider is created for it — that entity is the one knob, and a second control would only let the two disagree.

State-of-charge floors are pool-level for a reason: a pool's target is a capacity-weighted average, so a per-member floor would not mean anything. One knob writes the same value to every member.

Select

select.eb_<pool>_min_soc_mode balanced runs the floor algorithm with its safeguards; manual uses the manual floor slider verbatim, which is how you deliberately go below what the safeguards allow.

Compatibility sensors

A set of plainly named sensors exists for dashboard cards that expect a conventional layout: sensor.eb_solar_power, sensor.eb_home_consumption_power, sensor.eb_battery_power, sensor.eb_battery_soc, sensor.eb_ev_power, sensor.eb_autarky_rate and sensor.eb_self_consumption_rate. They carry the same values as the chain entities above, shaped the way generic energy cards expect.
